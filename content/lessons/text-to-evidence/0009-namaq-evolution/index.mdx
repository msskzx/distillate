## What changed

Namaq did not move in a straight line. It began by collecting many kinds of educational content,
then narrowed around people and their relationships. The graph pushed the project toward provenance;
provenance pushed it toward a source reader and reviewed claims; those claims became quiz material.
The latest commits move the centre again, toward Qur’an comparison, branch trees and shared recall.

The stable thread is not a feature. It is the attempt to turn source material into something that
can be **navigated, checked and remembered**.
