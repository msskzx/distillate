## Every automatic pair has to match

Suppose A matches B and B matches C. A connected component would put all three
on one card even if A and C barely agree. Namaq instead builds groups whose every pair matches, then checks every pair again after selecting shorter word spans. When
cropping invalidates a larger group, the builder retains the qualifying pairs.
The code that chooses the representation also runs its final membership check.
<RepoLink repo="namaq" path="src/lib/quran/mutashabih/engine.ts" />

Cited groups are retained as authored examples. The current evidence file
contains thirty book cases. Their citation records hold the particular places
the source compares, the edition, a short quote and a printed locator or section
link. Additional members of a group need their own basis for belonging.

## The thresholds belong to Namaq

The engine records exact spelling, same letters, same lemma and same root as
separate alignment levels. Exact and letter matches have weight eight, lemma
matches five, and root matches one in its weighted LCS. Exact, letter and lemma
matches count as strong matches for retrieval.

The current configuration requires six strong matches and 72% coverage for a
complete-ayah comparison. A local span requires eight matches, with at most
three words between successive strong matches. Unattributed shared phrases
require six words and no more than thirty-six places. These are the engine's
initial retrieval choices. The books supply the terminology and cited cases.
The configuration and classification order are in
<RepoLink repo="namaq" path="src/lib/quran/mutashabih/engine.ts" />.

Exact repetition remains a link kind. The book benchmark set also retains an
identical sub-ayah phrase as a shared phrase. Difference tags and link kinds
therefore answer separate questions. A group can carry several difference tags,
and the extra gender, noun/pronoun, case and frame tags identify Namaq's extensions.
<RepoLink repo="namaq" path="src/lib/quran/mutashabih/types.ts" />

## A selected phrase needs the right denominator

The selected spans for 2:4 and 4:162 cover eight words in each ayah. Comparing
those spans alone originally gave the group a perfect closeness score. The
whole ayat contain twelve and twenty-six words. The final alignment finds nine
strong matches over the complete ayat, so its coverage is 9/26, about 0.346.
For 2:48 and 2:123, the coverage is 17/19, about 0.895. “Closest first” now puts
the latter comparison ahead of the short shared phrase.
<RepoLink repo="namaq" path="src/app/quran/branches/mutashabih.json" />

The engine keeps the minimum whole-ayah coverage across every pair in a group.
The selected spans still decide the comparison evidence and highlighted words.
Changing the denominator for ranking does not require changing those spans.

## Evidence keeps its own scope

The owner specified the messenger group containing 2:129, 2:151, 3:164 and 62:2
as a regression example. Its Z03 citation names
2:129 and 2:151. The footer displays those cited places beside the edition
locator. A focused view that excludes the source's comparison hides that citation.
A reader can distinguish the displayed group from the particular comparison
supported by the book.
<RepoLink repo="namaq" path="src/app/quran/branches/GroupDetails.tsx" />

Tree and matrix are two views of the same group. The difference chip below the
card applies the funnel's corresponding filter and preserves the current group.
The differing expression sits beside it as plain text. The matching query,
selected view, scopes and sort order travel in the URL.

## The latest choice may be newer than the address

The browser can still show the old URL after a filter click. Namaq holds the
latest complete draft separately, so a second click uses the choices already
made. A navigation response only reconciles a pending draft when its filters
match. Browser back and forward deliberately restore the draft from the address.

Clearing a type and immediately adding book-only filtering exposed a second
requirement. Replacing only the book-only key preserved the cleared type from
the old URL. The control now deletes all managed filter keys before serializing
its complete draft. The test keeps the mocked address stale between the two
clicks, preserving the timing that produced the failure.
