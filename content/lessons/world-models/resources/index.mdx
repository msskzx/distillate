- [World Models (Ha & Schmidhuber, 2018)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.10122) — compact latent representations and imagined rollouts.
- [Dream to Control (Hafner et al., 2020)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.01603) — learning behavior by latent imagination.
- [MuZero (Schrittwieser et al., 2020)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-020-03051-4) — planning with a learned model that predicts planning-relevant quantities.
- [Dream-RSI (Zheng et al., 2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14858) — using accumulated discovery trees as replay simulators for recursive exploration-policy improvement.
