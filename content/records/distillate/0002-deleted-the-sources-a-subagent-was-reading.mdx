Four subagents converted namaq's lessons to MDX in parallel, each told to read the old HTML from
namaq's `docs/lessons/`. While they ran, the coordinator moved on to the namaq half of the move and
ran `git rm -r docs/lessons` in the same checkout. One subagent found its sources gone halfway through
lesson 0001. It recovered them from the commit before the deletion with `git archive` and finished, and
the text-parity check later showed nothing lost. A less careful agent would have reported "files not
found" or, worse, converted from memory.

**Evidence:** namaq commit `773a035d` removing `docs/lessons/`, made while the `text-to-evidence`
conversion was still running; that subagent's report of reading from `git archive 607669f6`.

**Implications:** a delegated task's inputs are frozen until it reports back. Hand a subagent a
snapshot (a commit hash, or a copy in the scratchpad), not a live path in a checkout someone else
is editing, and do not delete or move anything a running subagent was told to read.
