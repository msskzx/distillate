The record schema requires a `date` in `YYYY-MM-DD` form. The first check matched the
pattern and called `Date.parse`, treating `NaN` as invalid. The test for "not a real calendar
date" used `2026-13-45`, which every parser rejects, so it passed.

The reviewer tried `2026-02-30`. `Date.parse` returned a timestamp for March 2: the engine
rolls an overflowing day into the next month instead of refusing it. Any record could have
claimed a day that never happened.

The fix parses the string as UTC midnight and requires `toISOString().slice(0, 10)` to give
back the same string. A rolled-over date comes back different and fails.

**Evidence:** <RepoLink repo="distillate" path="lib/records.ts" /> (`checkRecord`), and the
tests for `2026-02-30` and `2026-04-31` in <RepoLink repo="distillate" path="lib/records.test.ts" />.

**Implications:** a date check is only as good as the cases it is shown to reject. Include a
day that overflows its month, since that is the case lenient parsers accept.
