The restyle moved every colour to theme tokens. The quiz's right and wrong answers became
`border-solved` and `border-failed`, and the suite failed:
`expected 'w-full rounded-sm border … border-solved text-solved' to match /green/`.
The test was named for what the reader sees ("marks the correct option green") but checked the
class string. Nothing the reader would notice had changed.

The owner had asked to keep tests as they were, so the quiz went back to Tailwind's green and red
utilities with dark variants, and the two quiz colours stay outside the token set.

**Evidence:** <RepoLink repo="distillate" path="components/mdx/Quiz.test.tsx" /> and
<RepoLink repo="distillate" path="components/mdx/Quiz.tsx" />.

**Implications:** a test of a visual state should assert the state the reader is given, here the ✓
and ✗ marks and the screen-reader text the component already renders. Rewriting this test that way
is the follow-up that lets the quiz take the palette.
