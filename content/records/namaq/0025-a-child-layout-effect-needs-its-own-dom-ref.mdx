The first curved-connector component used a ref supplied by its parent. Its layout effect ran before that ref was attached, returned early, and never registered a resize observer. The diagram mounted without any curved paths.

The child now starts from its own SVG ref and finds the containing list through its parent element. Geometry tests check that actual paths appear, then check direction, rotation and changed entries.

**Evidence:** [Namaq PR #520](https://github.com/msskzx/namaq/pull/520), <RepoLink repo="namaq" path="src/app/quran/branches/TreeLinks.tsx" /> and <RepoLink repo="namaq" path="src/app/quran/branches/TreeLinks.test.tsx" />.

**Implications:** mounting successfully does not establish that measurement or observation started. Check the resulting geometry, including a later resize or rotation.
