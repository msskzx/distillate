The Quran picker appeared bounded, but the page had a long blank tail. Its
scrollable list clipped the visible rows while the hidden radio inputs were
absolutely positioned relative to an ancestor outside those rows. Their positions
still enlarged the document's scrollable area: `document.documentElement.scrollHeight` reached 6,098px.
Changing the scroll wrapper alone did not fix it. Setting `position: relative` on
each label established the containing block needed by its input.

**Evidence:** [Namaq PR #516](https://github.com/msskzx/namaq/pull/516). <RepoLink repo="namaq" path="src/components/homepage/HomeQuranWidget.tsx" />
places each `sr-only` radio inside a label;
<RepoLink repo="namaq" path="src/components/homepage/HomeQuranWidget.module.css" />
sets `.row` to `position: relative` and keeps `.scroll` at `18rem` with
`overflow-y: auto`. The session's Playwright document-height check exposed the
layout failure. A one-off Playwright assertion required document height below
1,800px and passed at 390px and 1,440px widths in both themes after the fix.
That script was temporary; there is no committed browser regression test for
this layout. The jsdom selection tests in
<RepoLink repo="namaq" path="src/components/homepage/HomeQuranWidget.test.tsx" />
do not perform browser layout and could not detect it.

**Implications:** a list can have working selection and bounded visible content
while its hidden controls enlarge the document. Verify the document's vertical
extent in a browser when adding a long list of visually hidden inputs.
