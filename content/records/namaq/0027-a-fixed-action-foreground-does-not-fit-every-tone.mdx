The theme gave filled actions a constant fig-red background and warm-white labels in both modes. Selected connection chips also use the primary button treatment, but supply their own tone. Their dark-mode fills are pale, so the fixed warm-white foreground reduced contrast.

Independent review found the chip case. Explicit tones now supply the theme paper foreground, which is dark in dark mode. Default fig actions retain warm white. The video legend reads the same computed colors, so the correction also reaches exports.

**Evidence:** [Namaq PR #520](https://github.com/msskzx/namaq/pull/520), <RepoLink repo="namaq" path="src/components/common/Button.tsx" />, <RepoLink repo="namaq" path="src/app/theme.css" /> and <RepoLink repo="namaq" path="src/app/quran/branches/GrowVideo.tsx" />.

**Implications:** review a shared button's custom-tone callers alongside its default action. A readable brand button does not prove that selected semantic chips remain readable.
