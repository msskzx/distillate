The homepage reused the browse filter panel. Its controls still derived their
destination pathname from the current address, so selecting a relation there
navigated to `/?relations=...`. The homepage ignored that query. The filter looked
interactive without reaching any filtered results.

The fix sets the destination pathname to `/quran/branches` before serializing
the filters. The homepage also supplies the surah options needed by the reused
panel. Its route is part of the control's behavior even when another page hosts it.

**Evidence:** [Namaq PR #516](https://github.com/msskzx/namaq/pull/516). <RepoLink repo="namaq" path="src/app/quran/branches/GroupFilters.tsx" />
sets `url.pathname` explicitly. The regression in
<RepoLink repo="namaq" path="src/app/quran/branches/GroupFilters.test.tsx" />
starts at `/`, clicks the verbal-parallel filter, and checks both the branches
pathname and the relation parameter.
<RepoLink repo="namaq" path="src/components/homepage/Homepage.tsx" /> passes
`allSurahs` to FilterButton at source commit 6dae47a3.

**Implications:** reusing a control on a new page needs a check from that page.
An assertion about query parameters alone would have accepted the broken URL.
